Calibration
  Anything with a serial number is calibrated annually. The spectrum analyser
  RF-00417 and the logic analyser DG-00551 are both due in the same month, which
  is deliberate: the bench is unusable during either calibration, so they are
  scheduled together rather than spread out.

  The noise figure meter NF-88 is not calibrated by us. It returns to Delft with
  its own certificate and must not be sent to our calibration vendor.

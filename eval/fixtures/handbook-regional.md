# Orbital Dynamics - Regional Policy Supplements (fictional)

Regional supplements to the global handbook. Where a supplement and
the global handbook disagree, the global handbook governs.

## EMEA - Procurement thresholds

A purchase order above 4,000 EUR needs a second signature from the EMEA finance lead.

This supplement applies to staff whose employing entity is registered in EMEA. It does not apply to visitors or to staff on short-term assignment from another region.

## EMEA - Capital expenditure

Capex above 60,000 EUR goes to the quarterly review board rather than a line manager.

This supplement applies to staff whose employing entity is registered in EMEA. It does not apply to visitors or to staff on short-term assignment from another region.

## EMEA - Contractor engagement

A contractor engaged for more than 3 months is reclassified and needs a headcount slot.

This supplement applies to staff whose employing entity is registered in EMEA. It does not apply to visitors or to staff on short-term assignment from another region.

## EMEA - Data residency

EMEA customer records stay in the Frankfurt region and are not replicated outside it.

This supplement applies to staff whose employing entity is registered in EMEA. It does not apply to visitors or to staff on short-term assignment from another region.

## EMEA - Local holidays

EMEA observes 6 additional public holidays that are not in the global calendar.

This supplement applies to staff whose employing entity is registered in EMEA. It does not apply to visitors or to staff on short-term assignment from another region.

## EMEA - Expense receipts

Receipts are required above 30 EUR; below that a line item on the report is enough.

This supplement applies to staff whose employing entity is registered in EMEA. It does not apply to visitors or to staff on short-term assignment from another region.

## EMEA - Travel class

Flights over 4 hours may be booked in premium economy without prior approval.

This supplement applies to staff whose employing entity is registered in EMEA. It does not apply to visitors or to staff on short-term assignment from another region.

## EMEA - Equipment refresh
